Expertise

Where the work goes deep.

Four areas, built up over a decade of research and production work. They overlap more often than not — a forecasting system still needs pipelines, and a model nobody can explain is a model nobody will approve.

  1. Machine learning in production

    Systems that run reliably outside a notebook: data pipelines, training, evaluation, deployment and monitoring. The hard part is rarely the model — it is everything that has to keep working around it once real data starts arriving.

  2. MLOps and infrastructure

    Versioning, reproducibility, experiment tracking, and CI/CD for models. The tooling that decides whether a system is still maintainable a year after the first release, by people who did not build it.

  3. Time series and forecasting

    Demand, risk and signal problems on temporal data, from classical statistical methods through to deep architectures. Choosing between them on evidence rather than fashion is most of the work.

  4. Explainable AI

    Making model behaviour inspectable and defensible. This matters most where a decision has to be justified to somebody — a regulator, an auditor, or the person the decision was made about.
