About us

Research based. Engineering driven.

Pantelis Linardatos is the Founder of Linardatos AI. He is a Machine Learning Engineer and Researcher with a decade of experience bridging the gap between academic theory and industrial application.

He holds a PhD in Machine Learning, where his research focused on the mathematical optimization of algorithms. This strong theoretical foundation allows him to approach complex data problems with scientific precision.

Industry Experience

Beyond academia, Pantelis has extensive experience in the private sector, designing and deploying production-grade machine learning systems. He specializes in MLOps, cloud infrastructure, and scaling AI solutions that deliver consistent value.

Philosophy

Linardatos AI was founded to deliver engineering work that is technically sound, scalable, and accountable. Every system is built to be understood by the people who pay for it, run by the people who depend on it, and revisited by whoever inherits it next.
